Our philosophy

"Your community.
Your AI.
Your rules."

— jLabs manifesto

purpleGPT was born in a classroom — a group of students who wanted to see how far they could push a side project. No VC funding. No corporate roadmap. Just a team of curious people building something they actually want to use.

We believe AI should be embedded in your community — not a separate tab, not a chatbot you visit. It should know your server, speak your language, and stay out of the way when it's not needed.

Every feature ships because we thought it was cool. Every bug gets fixed because someone in the community reported it. That's it.
